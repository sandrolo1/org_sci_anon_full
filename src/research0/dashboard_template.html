    Step 4 · Data Extraction
    Construct pairs
    
    
    A pair sets one paper's use of a construct against another paper's. The signals and the model select which pairs reach the annotators; this page shows how they behave.
    
  
    
    
    What the signals are
      
    The pipeline measures how close two meanings are (S_def: definitions and meaning profile), how close their measures are (S_op), how many references the two papers share (C_cit), and puts the pair in a retrieval stratum (same label with diverging meanings, different labels with close meanings, close measures under distinct meanings, shifted context, hard negative, none). Earlier runs had one signal (S_sem-op) and jingle/jangle hints. These numbers order and sample the work; they are not verdicts, the thresholds are not calibrated, and a person decides.
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

    


    
  
    Human annotation · Constructs
    Construct annotation
    
    
    You decide what the relation between the two uses is. Scores and the pipeline's guess stay hidden while you annotate.
    
  
    
    
    Codebook v2: how to decide a pair (read before annotating)
      
      
        
    1. Compare the meanings: referent, defining properties, exclusions, level, time, theoretical role, scope.
      2. 
        
    2. Compare the measures: items, scales, informants, time window.
      3. 
        
    3. Substitution test: if one meaning replaced the other, or the two labels were merged, would a central proposition, explanation or prediction change?
      4. 
        
    4. Answer the three comparison questions, choose one class and write why, citing the pages.
      5. 
      
    
      
    
        
    Conceptual jingle
    One label, meanings that differ so that a proposition changes. Write the theoretical consequence.
    
        
    Conceptual jangle
    Different labels, same referent and compatible properties; substitution preserves the propositions. Write the theoretical consequence.
    
        
    Measurement divergence, scope variation
    Only the items or only the contexts differ.
    
        
    Empirical redundancy
    A high correlation or shared items alone.
    
        
    Same meaning
    Same label and same meaning: no fallacy.
    
      
    
      
    Scores, the pipeline's guess and the order of the pairs are hidden. Decisions taken with the earlier scale (relation + flag) are kept as history and those pairs come back. Nothing is merged by anyone but you.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

    



    
  
    Step 4 · Data Extraction
    Methodology cards
    
    
    What each paper declares about its question, data, design, measures and controls, with the paper's own words. The card describes; it does not judge the method.
    
  
    

    

    
  
    Step 4 · Data Extraction
    Impact cards
    
    
    What each paper declares about the conversation it joins and the change it claims to make, with the paper's own words. The card reports the claim, not the impact the paper had.
